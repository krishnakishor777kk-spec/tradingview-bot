## 1. Video 17: Intro to Chronos
### Foundations of Chronos Theory
* **Definition**: Chronos Theory is an interchangeable day-to-day market profiling system.
* **Core Concept**: Each trading day has a set amount of outcomes. Depending on the close of the current trading day, a correlated outcome is selected for the next trading day.
* **Daily Profiling Rule**: We always wait for the current day to close before conducting analysis, using that information to project the profile of the following day.
* **Weekly Application**: Chronos is primarily applicable to the **weekly cycle** (Monday through Friday), taking day-to-day data to forecast upcoming daily profiles.

### Monday's Function & The 3 Weekly Outcomes
Monday is the most important trading day of the week because it sets the tone and bias for the entire week. Monday can close in one of three outcomes:

#### Outcome 1: Monday Expansion Higher
* **Definition**: Monday trades and closes significantly higher.
* **Correlated Tuesday Profile**: Tuesday has a very high likelihood of continuing Monday's momentum (**Tuesday Continuation Profile**).
* **Trading Guidelines**: 
  * Expect the market to continue drawing higher on Tuesday.
  * Look for a Higher Time Frame (HTF) PD array to play off of.
  * Monday can serve as the **Timed True Reversal** and remain the absolute low of the week.
  * Alternatively, wait for a bullish crack in correlation during Tuesday's morning session.

#### Outcome 2: Monday Consolidation
* **Definition**: Monday remains range-bound and closes as a consolidation.
* **Correlated Tuesday Profile**: **Tuesday Aggression Profile**.
* **Key Rule**: **90% of the time**, a Monday consolidation range gets eliminated on **both sides** during the week.
* **Trading Guidelines**:
  * **Option A**: Sit out and wait for a Weekly Cycle SSMT (Sequential SMT) to form at Monday's High or Low to serve as a timed true reversal.
  * **Option B**: Target Monday's High or Low during the New York Morning Session of Tuesday, depending on the directional crack in correlation present.

#### Outcome 3: Monday Expansion Lower
* **Definition**: Monday trades and closes significantly lower.
* **Correlated Tuesday Profile**: Bearish continuation lower, but with an anticipation of a bullish **Intra-Week Reversal**.
* **Trading Guidelines**:
  * Tuesday is expected to follow lower until a bullish Timed True Reversal occurs.
  * Once the Timed True Reversal prints, expect a bullish intra-week reversal that allows price to trade higher for the rest of the week.
  * *Note*: Bearish continuations after a Monday expansion lower are generally short-lived. This is the least probable weekly scenario.

## 2. Video 18: Chronos Part 2
### Trading Tuesday & Its Dual Profiles
Depending on Monday's close, Tuesday will print either a Continuation or an Aggression profile.

#### Profile A: Tuesday Continuation Profile (Following Monday Expansion)
* **Mechanics**: Monday expands, and Tuesday continues the momentum in the same direction.
* **Key Tools**:
  * **Sequential Gaps**: Utilize 6-Hour ($H6$) and 4-Hour ($H4$) sequential gaps either as support for a crack in correlation or as direct entry points.
  * **Protected Price Runs**: Expect Monday's low (in a bullish week) or Monday's high (in a bearish week) to be a protected low/high. This provides a highly reliable area for stop-loss placement.
  * **Timed True Reversal**: When Monday serves as the low of the week, it acts as the Timed True Reversal, allowing clean continuation.

#### Profile B: Tuesday Aggression Profile (Following Monday Consolidation)
* **Mechanics**: Monday consolidates; Tuesday aggressively targets the boundaries of Monday's range.
* **Trading Guidelines**:
  * Wait for the **New York Morning Session** (EST).
  * Utilize **Working TPDs (Terminus Price Divergence)** on the 4-Hour ($4h$), 1-Hour ($1h$), and 15-Minute ($15m$) charts within their proper time windows.
  * Align TPDs with SMT/TPD Time Alignment. Look for specific sequential SMTs to lead to one side of the market, using Monday's high/low as a primary target.
  * **Pre-Session Setup (London Session)**: If London session takes out Monday's high/low, creating a Weekly Cycle crack in correlation before the NY session:
    * Wait for the NY Morning Session.
    * Seek a second-stage crack in correlation in agreement with the weekly cycle sequential SMT.
    * Target the London session low/high alongside Monday's low/high.

### Tuesday's Function & Wednesday Projections
Tuesday's close provides critical feedback on whether the weekly cycle is continuing in a steady, high-probability order. Tuesday's close yields three distinct outcomes:

1. **Tuesday Expansion Continuation**: Tuesday continues Monday's expansion. This confirms a highly stable weekly trend. Expect Wednesday to continue the price run (the "easy" weeks where Monday remains the low/high of the week).
2. **Tuesday Reversal**: Tuesday forms an opposing crack in correlation (either on the monthly cycle or against Monday's high/low, e.g., via a London sweep). Expect Wednesday to continue the reversal.
3. **Tuesday Consolidation**: Tuesday attempts to continue the run but fails, retracing and closing back inside the range as a consolidation. This triggers a **Restabilization Protocol**—Wednesday must be treated with the exact same rules as a Monday consolidation.

## 3. Video 19: Chronos Part 3
### Trading Wednesday: The Centerpiece of the Week
Wednesday is a unique trading day because it is the optimal day for high-probability intraday setups, regardless of Tuesday's directional influence.

#### Core Rules for Wednesdays:
* **Morning Session Focus**: Wednesday is best traded exclusively in the **New York Morning Session** (EST).
* **High/Low of the Day**: The NY Morning session creates the absolute high or low of the day on Wednesday more times than not. This session holds the main expansion of the day.
* **Already Expanded Rule**: If price has already expanded significantly prior to the morning session (e.g., during Asia/London), expect a **Retracement Profile** during the NY Morning session to establish the high/low.
* **Prominence of Setups**: Wednesday prints the cleanest and most reliable **crack in correlation** setups of the entire week, including:
  * Two-Stage Sequential SMTs
  * Order of Operations setups
  * Time-specific TPDs aligned with a crack in correlation
  * True Open Rules
  * Daily Cycle and 15-Minute Cycle alignments
  * *Note*: These setups are far less reliable or prominent on Mondays and Tuesdays compared to Wednesdays.

### Wednesday's Function & Weekly Halving Splits
The primary function of Wednesday is to **confirm the weekly halving split** (as most weeks follow a 3/2 or 2/3 split structure). Wednesday acts as the pivot point that confirms whether a low/high of the week is already locked in or if a new one is forming.

#### Low of the Week (LOTW) Profiles:
1. **Wednesday Continuation of Tuesday Expansion (Monday LOTW)**: Monday expands higher $\rightarrow$ Tuesday continues higher $\rightarrow$ Wednesday continues higher. Once Wednesday closes higher, it confirms Monday is the LOTW.
2. **Wednesday Continuation of Tuesday Reversal (Tuesday LOTW)**: Monday consolidates $\rightarrow$ Tuesday sweeps below Monday's low to create a crack in correlation $\rightarrow$ Wednesday continues higher. Confirms Tuesday is the LOTW.
3. **Wednesday Reversal of Tuesday Reversal (Wednesday LOTW)**: Monday expands lower $\rightarrow$ Tuesday continues lower $\rightarrow$ Wednesday prints a bullish crack in correlation and expands higher. Confirms Wednesday is the LOTW.

#### High of the Week (HOTW) Profiles:
1. **Wednesday Continuation of Tuesday Reversal of Monday Expansion (Tuesday HOTW)**: Monday expands higher $\rightarrow$ Tuesday prints an opposing crack in correlation and reverses lower $\rightarrow$ Wednesday continues lower, following Tuesday's reversal momentum. Confirms Tuesday is the HOTW.
2. **Wednesday Reversal of Tuesday Consolidation (Wednesday HOTW)**: Monday prints any outcome $\rightarrow$ Tuesday consolidates $\rightarrow$ Wednesday sweeps Tuesday's high, prints a bearish crack in correlation, and expands lower. Confirms Wednesday is the HOTW.

## 4. Video 20: Chronos Part 4
### Trading Thursday: The Second Half Pivot
By the time Wednesday closes, you will know with high probability whether the weekly high or low is locked in. This allows you to trade Thursday directly toward the second half's anticipated pivot point.

#### General Setup:
* **If LOTW/HOTW is Locked**: E.g., if a bullish Timed True Reversal occurred in the first half (Monday or Tuesday low) and Wednesday closed higher, you know the low is locked. Thursday's target is the current high of the week, expanding to create a new weekly high.
* **Intraday Thursday Profiles**:
  1. **Retracement Profile in the Morning**: If early Thursday (Asia/London) price has already expanded and taken out the weekly high/low (creating the new high/low in the second half), expect a retracement profile in the NY Morning. Look for a bearish (or bullish) 90-minute or daily cycle sequential SMT to trade price back into the range.
  2. **Expansion Profile in the Morning**: If price has *not yet* created the new weekly high/low, expect an expansion profile. Price will typically hover just below the weekly high (bullish) or just above the weekly low (bearish) prior to 9:30 AM EST or high-impact news. Use time alignment and working TPDs (90-minute, daily cycle, 4h, 1h TPDs) to frame a crack in correlation and trade the expansion toward a new weekly high/low.

### Thursday's Function
* **The 50/50 Rule**: Thursday is the only day of the week that **lacks a strong directional bias function**. The probability of Thursday reversing the weekly range versus continuing it is **near even (50/50)**.
* **Navigating the 50/50 Probability**: 
  * Look for a weekly cycle sequential SMT between Wednesday and Thursday, or a multi-stage crack in correlation in the Thursday morning session to signal a reversal of the weekly range.
  * If no crack in correlation or reversal signature is present, default to expecting Thursday to continue in the direction of the established weekly range. Do not guess a reversal simply because "it is Thursday."

## 5. Video 21: Chronos Part 5
### Trading Fridays: Closing the Weekly Range
Friday's primary profile is determined by how Thursday closes.

* **Friday Continuation**: If Thursday closed continuing the weekly range (e.g., breaking new highs and expanding higher without any reversal signatures), Friday will continue that momentum.
* **Friday Reversal**: If a weekly cycle sequential SMT formed between Wednesday and Thursday, and Thursday closed in the direction of that reversal crack in correlation, expect Friday to continue the weekly reversal momentum.
* **Intraday Strategy**: Utilize the two standard daily profiles: **Continuation Profile** or **Reversal Profile**.
* **The "Return to Range" Myth**: While there is a common theory that Friday retraces back into the weekly range (New York Retracement), this **only occurs if a crack in correlation confirms it** during the morning session. Daily profiles override general day-of-week theories. If no reversal signature prints, expect standard New York session expansion.

### Chronos Time Extension: Optimal Trading Times (The "When")
Chronos Time Extension combines market narrative with the day of the week to pinpoint the exact session (EST) to execute once you have direction and bias.

#### Buying Cheat Sheet (Longs):
* **Monday**: Buy Asia or NY Morning. (Equal probability of creating the low of the day. If an HTF gap/level is present, buying Asia is viable, expecting the low to hold).
* **Tuesday**: Buy Asia **only if** Monday printed a Quarter Expansion Higher (offering a 4h FVG continuation). If this condition is not met, wait and buy the NY Morning Session.
* **Wednesday**: Buy Asia **only if** Tuesday printed a Quarter Expansion Higher (offering a 4h or 6h FVG in support of order flow, or Tuesday printed an intra-week reversal). Otherwise, wait for the NY Morning Session (highly recommended for Wednesdays).
* **Thursday**: Buy NY Morning Session (low of the day typically forms here).
* **Friday**: Buy NY Morning Session (low of the day typically forms here).

#### Selling Cheat Sheet (Shorts):
* **Monday**: **DO NOT SELL/SHORT AT ALL**. Bearish setups have a massive failure rate on Mondays, even in heavily bearish market conditions. Skip Monday shorts entirely and look to enter on Tuesday.
* **Tuesday**: Sell NY Morning Session (high of the day typically forms here).
* **Wednesday**: Sell NY Morning Session (high of the day typically forms here).
* **Thursday**: Sell Asia **only if** Wednesday printed a Quarter Expansion Lower (offering a bearish 4h FVG and bias is bearish). Otherwise, sell the NY Morning Session.
* **Friday**: Sell Asia **only if** Thursday printed a Quarter Expansion Lower (offering a bearish 4h or 6h FVG). Otherwise, sell the NY Morning Session.

*Note*: If the cheat sheet permits an Asia entry based on quarter expansion, but no clean HTF gap or level is present in Asia, default to waiting for the New York Morning Session.

## 6. Video 22: Psychology of Trading
Mastering the technical framework of Chronos is useless without mastering the psychological landscape of probabilities.

### The Percentage Paradigm Shift
* **The Reality of Setup Outcomes**: Every single trade has a unique, independent, and individual outcome, regardless of the historical success of the setup or the current market circumstances.
* **Perfect Setups Fail**: If markets were perfect, reoccurring setups would work 100% of the time. They do not. A phenomenal HTF setup can fail, and a mediocre, low-quality setup can trade beautifully.
* **The Role of Signatures**: Market signatures do not guarantee a win; they simply indicate that price has a **higher probability** of drawing in one direction over another.

### The Coin Flip Example (50% Win Rate)
To understand how probabilities distribute over time, consider flipping a coin 10 times in a row with a true 50% win rate:
* A 50% win rate does **not** alternate perfectly (Win, Loss, Win, Loss, Win, Loss...).
* It distributes randomly, often resulting in streaks: `H, T, T, H, T, T, H, H, T, H` (5 wins, 5 losses).
* This random distribution of outcomes triggers two highly destructive psychological traps in traders:

#### Trap 1: The Reckless Trading Inducer (Following a Losing Streak)
* **Trigger**: A trader experiences 3 or 4 consecutive losses within a strategy that has a profitable, validated win rate over time.
* **Psychological Reaction**: The trader personalizes the losses, thinking: *"I'm doing something wrong," "I'm stupid," "I can't make this work."*
* **Behavioral Consequence**: In emotional distress, they abandon discipline, ignore risk parameters, and "full-port" (over-leverage) the next trade out of frustration just to "get it over with." This destroys their account right before the winning streak of the distribution occurs.

#### Trap 2: The Euphoric Risk Inducer (Following a Winning Streak)
* **Trigger**: A trader experiences 3 or 4 consecutive wins in a row.
* **Psychological Reaction**: The trader feels invincible, thinking: *"I'm perfect," "I win all the time," "The market can't stop me."*
* **Behavioral Consequence**: They enter a state of euphoria, drastically increase their risk size on the next trade because they "know" it will win, only to suffer a single loss that wipes out all the profits from the winning streak and more.

### Separating the Trader from the Person
* **The Identity Trap**: Many traders subconsciously link their trading results to their self-worth. If they have a red day, they feel like a failure as a person; if they have a green day, they feel like a king.
* **External Pressures**: This identity trap is exacerbated by external life pressures: hating a job, desperation to escape a tight financial situation, wanting to help a struggling family member, or trying to prove themselves to family/friends who doubted them.
* **The Solution**: Realize that **progress in trading is non-linear**. A single win does not mean you are moving forward, and a single loss does not mean you are taking a step backward.
* **The Sample Size Rule**: The only way to measure if a strategy (and your execution) is successful is by analyzing a **large enough performance sample size** (e.g., 3 months, 6 months, 1 year). Do not let daily fluctuations dictate your emotions or your personal life.

### What Keeps You Afloat?
* **The Illusion of Control**: You have zero control over the outcome of the next trade, and you have zero control over the random distribution of wins and losses.
* **The One Element of Control**: The **only** element in the entire world of trading that you have absolute control over is **Risk Management**.
* **Risk Management as Mental Capital Protection**: Proper risk management does not just protect your account balance; it protects your **mental capital**. It allows you to maintain comfort and emotional neutrality in the face of market uncertainty.
* **The Rule of Survival**: Manage risk strictly according to what your mind can handle without emotional reaction. This keeps you afloat long enough to let probabilities, compounding, and time do the heavy lifting of building wealth.
