## 1. Entering a Trading Week
Every week, the trader establishes a directional baseline utilizing **Weekly Halving Theory** and **Chronos Theory** to dictate *when* and *where* to look for setups.

* **Weekly Framework**: Define a loose directional bias for the week:
  * *Example (Bullish)*: Expect the Low of the Week (LOW) to form in the **First Half** (Monday–Wednesday) and the High of the Week (HOW) to form in the **Second Half** (Thursday–Friday).
* **Monday's Profile**: Monday's actual price action (Chronos profile) is the filter that confirms or refines the weekly framework. 
  * If a Higher Timeframe (HTF) liquidity pool rests below market price, and Monday sweeps it, Monday's profile sets the weekly LOW, shifting the focus to buying continuations.

---

## 2. Interpreting Information & Daily Profiles
To frame a position, the chart must mathematically prove your weekly thesis through a **crack in correlation** (SMT) and **time alignment**. Do not trade on sentiment or feelings. Depending on Monday's close, there are three core profile scenarios:

### Profile A: Standard Continuation Days (Easy)
* **Context**: Monday expands strongly. Tuesday is anticipated as a continuation day.
* **Execution**: Asia session entries can be framed directly off **HTF Sequential Gaps** from Monday. These are the highest-probability, lowest-friction trading days.

### Profile B: Re-stabilization Days / Monday Consolidation (Moderate)
* **Context**: Monday remains range-bound (consolidation). 
* **Rules**: 
  1. Monday has a <10% chance of holding both the high and low of the week. Typically, both sides will get run out.
  2. Tuesday's direction is highly ambiguous. 
* **Execution**: Treat Tuesday as a **Reversal Day**. Look for a crack in correlation (SMT/TPD) to form at Monday's High or Low. If Tuesday validates this reversal, Wednesday is traded as an easy continuation day.

### Profile C: Monday Expanding Lower (Least Probable/Harder)
* **Context**: Monday expands lower (when the macro bias is bullish).
* **Rules**: Tuesdays and Wednesdays are expected to continue lower *until* a bullish Timed True Reversal (TTR) prints. 
* **Execution**: Follow Chronos Time Extension for morning session shorts, but remain alert for an HTF crack in correlation that marks the TTR and reverses the week bullishly.

## 3. Pulling the Trigger (Execution Models)
Once bias and daily narrative are aligned, execution relies on **Chronos Time Extension** to pinpoint the entry window, using static entry models:

* **Chronos Time Extension Rules**:
  * **Tuesday Bearish** $\rightarrow$ Monitor for a **Morning Session Sell Setup**.
  * **Thursday Bullish** $\rightarrow$ Monitor for a **Morning Session Buy Setup**.
* **Core Entry Models**:
  * Daily Cycle SSMT (Sequential SMT).
  * H1 TPD aligned with an M15 Reversion Level (RL) and a Lower Timeframe Market Maker Model (MMXM).
* **Key Open Profiles**:
  
  | Open Profile Type | Description | Trigger Model |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **Sequential Gap Open** | Price opens near a sequential gap from the previous day. | Direct entry during the Asia session. |
  | **Retrace Profile** | Price opens above the True Day Open & True Market Open. | Wait for the 9:30 AM open to pull back to the Reversion Level (RL), print a crack in correlation (90M or Daily SMT), and retrace back higher inside the range. |
  | **Immediate Expansion Profile** | Price lays flat, opening right at both True Opens. | Expect immediate expansion at the 9:30 AM EST open. Execute on a lower timeframe crack in correlation. |
  | **Counter-Bias Open (Trap)** | Price is above both True Opens, showing a retrace setup, but the daily bias is strongly Bearish. | **Do not buy the retrace.** Wait for the morning session to put in the High of the Day via a bearish crack in correlation, then enter short. |

## 4. Repeating the Process (The Trading Formula)
Trading is a repetitive, probabilistic business. Successful execution is governed by a strict lifecycle:

```
[1. Wait for Daily Close] ──► [2. Determine Chronos Narrative] ──► [3. Position Entry via Models] ──► [4. Journal & Reset]
```

* **No Setup = No Trade**: If a day closes with zero directional clarity, sit on your hands and wait for the next day's close.
* **Psychological Acceptance**: Not every day is tradeable, and not every week has clean profiles. When market profiles break down into chaotic consolidations, preserve capital, record the behavior, and reset.
