## 1. Video 1: Quarterly Theory Basics

### A. The 90-Minute Algorithmic Cycle
Quarterly Theory is built on the principle that time in the financial markets is fractal and structured in 90-minute quarters. Within any 24-hour day, price is delivered through four major 6-hour sessions, each divided into four 90-minute quarters:

| Session | Quarter | Time Window (EST) | Typical Algorithmic Behavior (AMD-X) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LONDON** | Q1 | 12:00 AM – 1:30 AM | **Accumulation**: Sets the initial session range. |
| | Q2 | 1:30 AM – 3:00 AM | **Manipulation**: Sweeps Q1 high/low (often sets London Low/High). |
| | Q3 | 3:00 AM – 4:30 AM | **Distribution**: Expands in the true direction of the London run. |
| | Q4 | 4:30 AM – 6:00 AM | **X (Reversal/Continuation)**: Distributes further or begins retracement. |
| **NY_AM** | Q1 | 6:00 AM – 7:30 AM | **Accumulation**: Pre-market range consolidation. |
| | Q2 | 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM | **Manipulation**: Pre-market sweeps / News-driven manipulation. |
| | Q3 | 9:00 AM – 10:30 AM | **Distribution**: 9:30 AM Equities open expansion in true direction. |
| | Q4 | 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM | **X (Reversal/Continuation)**: Late morning expansion or retracement. |
| **NY_PM** | Q1 | 12:00 PM – 1:30 PM | **Accumulation**: Lunch hour range consolidation. |
| | Q2 | 1:30 PM – 3:00 PM | **Manipulation**: PM Session sweep of lunch range / morning range. |
| | Q3 | 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM | **Distribution**: PM expansion (often includes bond close / final hour push). |
| | Q4 | 4:30 PM – 6:00 PM | **X (Reversal/Continuation)**: Session wrap-up or overnight transition. |
| **ASIA** | Q1 | 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM | **Accumulation**: Early Asian session range. |
| | Q2 | 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM | **Manipulation**: Asia sweep of initial range. |
| | Q3 | 9:00 PM – 10:30 PM | **Distribution**: Main Asian session expansion. |
| | Q4 | 10:30 PM – 12:00 AM | **X (Reversal/Continuation)**: Pre-London range consolidation. |

### B. The AMD-X Cycle Mechanics
Every quarter cycle has a specific institutional role:
1.  **Accumulation (A)**: Smart money accumulates positions by keeping price in a tight range.
2.  **Manipulation (M)**: Price is driven sharply outside the accumulation range to trigger retail stop-losses and induce traders to take the wrong direction.
3.  **Distribution (D)**: The true expansion of the session. Price trends rapidly toward target liquidity.
4.  **X (Reversal or Continuation)**: If the distribution target is hit, Q4 acts as a reversal back into the range. If the target is not yet reached, Q4 continues the expansion.

## 2. Video 2: Time Alignment

### A. The Structural Time Trigger
High-probability entries do not happen at random times. Price must align with **Time**.
*   **The Equities Open Window**: The most critical time alignment window is **9:30 AM – 10:00 AM EST**.
*   **Time Alignment Gaps**: The entry triggers are strictly checked at key time intervals:
    *   **8:45 AM EST**: Look for a **15-Minute FVG/Gap**.
    *   **9:15 AM EST**: Look for a **5-Minute FVG/Gap**.
    *   **9:30 AM EST**: Look for a **1-Minute FVG/Gap** as equities open.
*   **The Execution Rule**: If a setup forms outside of these algorithmic quarters or time alignment windows, it is a low-probability trade and must be ignored.

## 3. Video 3: Narrative

### A. Identifying the Draw on Liquidity (DOL)
Narrative is the "story" of the market based on Higher Timeframes (HTF) like the Daily or H4 charts. Before trading, you must identify:
1.  **Where is price going?** This is the **Draw on Liquidity (DOL)**. Price always seeks either:
    *   **Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)**: Previous daily highs, session highs, or equal highs.
    *   **Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)**: Previous daily lows, session lows, or equal lows.
    *   **Imbalances**: Unmitigated Fair Value Gaps (FVG) or Volume Imbalances on Higher Timeframes.
2.  **Premium vs. Discount**:
    *   If the higher timeframe narrative is **Bullish**, you only buy when price pulls back into a **Discount** (below 50% of the current HTF swing range).
    *   If the higher timeframe narrative is **Bearish**, you only sell when price retraces into a **Premium** (above 50% of the current HTF swing range).

## 4. Video 4: TPD's (Terminus Price Divergence) Basics

### A. The 3-Candle Structural Transition
Terminus Price Divergence (TPD) represents the transition of institutional order flow. It is a strictly time-locked sequence consisting of three candles:
1.  **Candle 1 (Reference Range)**: Sets the structural benchmark or range.
2.  **Candle 2 (Sweep/Manipulation)**: Sweeps either the high or low of Candle 1, trapping liquidity.
3.  **Candle 3 (Displacement/Reversal)**: Reverses direction and begins the primary expansion.

### B. Introducing the Reversion Level
*   During Candle 2's formation, a lower timeframe CSD (Change in State of Delivery) is created in the second half of its duration.
*   This specific level is the **Reversion Level**. Price is mathematically drawn to revert to this level during Candle 3's formation before expanding.
*   By placing limit orders directly at the Reversion Level immediately after Candle 2 closes, you can capture the exact turning point without waiting for Candle 3's close.

## 5. Video 5: A Deeper Dive Into SMT

### A. SMT Divergence Mechanics
SMT (Smart Money Technique) is a divergence that occurs between highly correlated assets at key swing highs/lows.
*   **Bullish SMT**:
    *   Asset A sweeps the low of a previous swing point.
    *   Asset B **fails** to sweep that low (creates a higher low/failure swing).
    *   *Interpretation*: Asset B is showing institutional strength. It is the **Failure Swing Asset**.
*   **Bearish SMT**:
    *   Asset A sweeps the high of a previous swing point.
    *   Asset B **fails** to sweep that high (creates a lower high/failure swing).
    *   *Interpretation*: Asset B is showing institutional weakness. It is the **Failure Swing Asset**.

### B. Core Execution Rules
1.  **Always execute on the Failure Swing Asset**: The asset that failed to sweep is stronger (in a long) or weaker (in a short) and will expand much faster and cleaner.
2.  **Stop Loss Placement**: Place your stop loss strictly at the invalidation level, which is the swing high/low of Candle 2 on the Failure Swing Asset (plus a buffer).
3.  **Local SMT Validation**: The Failure Swing Asset must pullback to at least 50% discount (for buys) or 50% premium (for sells) of its local swing range to be valid.

## 6. Video 6: Framing Each Day

### A. Daily Profiling Checklist
To frame each trading day, you must run through this strict pre-market checklist:
1.  **Establish Directional Bias (Directional Foundation)**: Look at the previous day's close. Determine whether we are seeking macro BSL or SSL.
2.  **Locate Key Levels**: Mark the previous day's high/low, session highs/lows, and active HTF FVGs.
3.  **Identify Quarters & News**: Check the economic calendar for today's high-impact news times (to align volatility) and note the Quarter transitions.
4.  **Confirm the Execution Plan**: Wait for price to enter a premium/discount level, look for a crack in correlation (SMT) during the 9:30 AM open window, and execute at the reversion level using a limit order.
